Momentum Mascot
Features Install GitHub

A tiny room on your desktop for the projects you keep coming back to.

Momentum Mascot is a retro pixel character who watches your side-project commits and simply feels what is going on. Commit and they work. Drift and they doze, then sleep. Come back after a long silence and they leap out of bed.

The mascot never dies. It waits.

Awake

Four moods, one commit at a time

It reads exactly one thing from each repo: when you last committed. No messages, no diffs, no scores.

Awake

Last commit under 24 hours. They are at their desk, working.

Dozing

24 to 72 hours. Standing away with coffee, getting quiet.

Asleep

Three days or more. In bed, still holding your place.

Comeback

A real commit after a sleep. They leap out of bed to celebrate.

Lives in the corner

A 64×64 desktop pet floats over your workspace as a small ambient signal. Click it to open the room. Drag it to snap it into another corner.

Awake

Share without leaking

Copy a 1200×630 card to your clipboard. It carries the mood and the room — and deliberately nothing that identifies a project: no names, paths, messages, hashes, or timestamps.

Install

  1. Download Momentum Mascot.dmg from the latest release.
  2. Open the DMG and drag the app to Applications.
  3. First launch: right-click the app and choose Open, because the build is ad-hoc signed and not notarized.
  4. Or run this in Terminal: 
    xattr -d com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Momentum Mascot.app"

The app does not start on login yet. After a restart, open it yourself.

Privacy is the architecture

  • No network requests — ever.
  • No accounts, no sign-in, no telemetry.
  • No cloud, no sync, no hosted component.
  • Everything lives in ~/.keepgoing/mascot/state.json, which you can read or delete.

What this is not

Momentum Mascot is not a productivity tool. It will not score you, streak you, nudge you, or tell you how long it has been since your last commit. It is a small companion for people with demanding day jobs who go through long stretches where nothing gets committed because life is happening.
